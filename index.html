Again?! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Again?! is published by Pomp. This policy explains what the app accesses, what stays on your device, and what is processed by purchase providers.

Request data deletion

Information processed on your device

With your permission, the Again?! app reads Android app-usage history to detect when apps you select are opened. The app stores the following locally:

  • Selected app package names and display names
  • Open attempts, detected opens, timestamps, and session durations
  • Daily usage summaries
  • Monitoring, notification, reminder, theme, roast-style, and language preferences

Again?! does not inspect what you type, view, post, or send inside other apps. App-usage history and monitored-app activity are not sent to Pomp, RevenueCat, advertisers, or other third parties.

How local information is used

Local information is used to:

  • Detect openings of selected apps
  • Display intervention overlays and reminders
  • Build the daily usage receipt
  • Restore monitoring after a device or app restart
  • Apply your selected settings

Again?! has no user accounts, advertising, or cloud synchronization.

Purchases and third-party processing

Again?! offers optional one-time purchases. Google Play processes payments. The app does not receive or store payment-card details.

RevenueCat provides purchase and entitlement management. When the RevenueCat SDK is configured, RevenueCat may process purchase history, Google Play purchase tokens, transaction identifiers, entitlement status, last app-use time, device type, and operating-system information. This information is used to provide and restore paid features and for purchase analytics. It is encrypted in transit.

RevenueCat does not receive monitored-app activity or Android app-usage history from the Again?! app.

For more information, see:

  • RevenueCat privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy

Feedback and support

Again?! can open your email app so you can contact Pomp. Sending an email is optional and user initiated. Your email provider processes the address, message, and attachments you choose to send. Pomp uses support messages only to reply, investigate problems, and improve the app.

Request data deletion

You can ask Pomp, the publisher of Again?!, to delete personal data associated with your use of the app. Again?! has no user accounts. You do not need to reinstall the app to send a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email againappdev@gmail.com with the subject Again?! data deletion request.
  2. Tell us whether you want purchase-related data, support messages, or both deleted. If your request concerns a purchase, include your Google Play order ID if available. For support messages, use the email address you previously contacted us from.
  3. We may ask for limited additional information to locate the correct records and verify that they belong to you. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

We handle deletion requests without undue delay and within 60 days of receipt, or sooner where required by law. Where the GDPR applies, we respond within one month and complete erasure without undue delay. If a lawful extension is necessary because of the complexity or number of requests, we notify you within the first month and explain the reason. If we cannot complete a deletion, we will explain which records remain, the reason, and the applicable retention period.

Data we delete

  • The RevenueCat customer record associated with your request, including associated purchase history, identifiers and entitlement information that can be deleted through our purchase-management provider.
  • Support correspondence held by Pomp that you ask us to delete, including your email address, message content and attachments, except any records we must retain as explained below.

Data that may be retained

Some transaction or request records may need to be retained to meet legal or accounting obligations, prevent fraud, or resolve an ongoing dispute. Any such records are kept only for the applicable required period. We will identify any exception and its retention period in our reply.

Google Play maintains its own payment and transaction records under Google's privacy policy. A request to Pomp does not delete your Google account or Google's independently held records. RevenueCat's retention practices are described in its privacy policy. Data deletion does not issue a purchase refund.

Remove data stored on your phone

Monitored-app activity, usage summaries and app preferences stay on your phone; Pomp cannot erase them remotely. To remove them, open Android Settings > Apps > Again?! > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall Again?!. Settings labels may vary by device. Clearing local data does not delete purchase data held by RevenueCat or Google Play.

Again?! is not directed to children under 13.
